Han Fang (方瀚)

I am currently a Research Scientist at the Xingchen AGI Lab, China Telecom Artificial Intelligence Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd., where I focus on developing generative AI and advancing its applications in downstream domains. As a core contributor, I led the research and development of the 'Xinghe' Vision Model series, the text-to-image model TeleImage-1.0, and the multimodal large model TeleMM-2.0.

My research aims to bridge the gap between vision and language by enabling models to perform vision-centric reasoning, with a focus on long video understanding, free-form multimodal grounding, and vision-language pretraining.

I am open to academic collaborations and welcome motivated research interns. Please feel free to reach out at fanghan1996@outlook.com.

方瀚

我目前在中电信人工智能科技（北京）有限公司星辰 AGI 实验室工作，主要关注生成式人工智能及其在实际场景中的落地应用。作为核心研发成员，我参与并主导了 “星河”视觉模型系列、文生图模型 TeleImage-1.0 和多模态大模型 TeleMM-2.0 的研发。

我的研究围绕视觉与语言的深度融合展开，重点关注模型在视觉信息上的理解、定位与推理能力。近期方向包括长视频理解、开放式多模态定位和视觉-语言预训练。

欢迎对相关方向感兴趣的同学和研究者交流合作，也欢迎对科研感兴趣的同学联系我：fanghan1996@outlook.com。
