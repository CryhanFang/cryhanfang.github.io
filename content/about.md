# Han Fang (方瀚)

I am a Research Scientist at the [Xingchen AGI Lab at China Telecom Artificial Intelligence Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd.](https://www.teleai.com.cn/), where I work on generative AI research and applications. As a core member of the research team, I played a leading role in developing the **Xinghe series of vision models**, the text-to-image model **TeleImage-1.0**, and the Xingchen multimodal model **TeleMM-2.0**.

My research focuses on multimodal representation learning, understanding, and reasoning grounded in visual evidence. My work spans fine-grained visual perception and region understanding, long video understanding and spatiotemporal reasoning, and visual agents. I also contribute to the development of foundation models, including multimodal large language models (MLLMs) and visual grounding models.

I am looking for motivated research interns and am also open to academic collaborations. Please feel free to reach out at [fanghan1996@outlook.com](mailto:fanghan1996@outlook.com).
